- New mode, Avoid Folds: the curve slowly changes shape. Latch onto a square and keep it as long as you can. When
  your square drifts towards another one, it turns red, because the two are about to merge and vanish. Once it's
  gone you have three seconds to find another.
- The game opens on a level menu. Each level is either Square Hunt or Avoid Folds, and each mode also has a random
  curve option.
