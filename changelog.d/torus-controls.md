- Two new controls on the control button, Torus pad and Dials, plus a torus map under the curve with every control.
  The map's two coordinates are two points on the curve, and the square stands on those two points: slide one finger
  anywhere to move the dot on the map, or turn one dial per point. Squares you have found show on the map, each at
  eight places, and the dashed diagonal is where the two points meet.
